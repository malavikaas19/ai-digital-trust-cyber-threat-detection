QR Code Safety Guide

QR codes can contain links or text. Check their contents before taking action.

01
Verify the Source
Be cautious with QR codes from unknown posters, messages or websites.
02
Inspect Extracted Links
Check the domain carefully before visiting an unfamiliar website.
03
Avoid Sensitive Requests
Do not enter passwords or payment details through unexpected QR links.
04
Verify Independently
Use an organisation's official website when checking important requests.
